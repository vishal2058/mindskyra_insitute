⚡ Don't ignore Class XI.
Class XI chapters are easier to crack and give you 20 guaranteed marks with far less effort. Prioritize them for quick wins especially in Oscillations, Laws of Motion, and Basic Organic Chemistry.
🎉 NO NEGATIVE MARKING in MHT CET 2026 — Attempt every single question. Even a random guess gives you a chance at marks!
MHT CET is a speed + accuracy exam. 150 questions in 180 minutes means 72 seconds per question. The syllabus closely follows Maharashtra State Board (MSBSHSE) — HSC textbooks are your bible. Application-based numericals dominate Physics and Maths; Chemistry rewards NCERT-style reading.
⚡ Physics — Blueprint
▸Kinetic Theory + Radiation = 12% — your highest single-chapter yield
▸Electrostatics = 3–4 Qs per shift — consistently most asked
▸Superposition of Waves = 4–5 Qs last year — huge ROI
▸Easy chapters (Semiconductors, Dual Nature) = 4 free marks
▸Class XI: Gravitation + Laws of Motion = 6–8 marks with low effort
🧪 Chemistry — Blueprint
▸HSC textbook line-by-line = covers 70–80% of Chemistry
▸p-Block Elements = 10% — highest chapter yield in Inorganic
▸Thermodynamics + Kinetics + Electrochemistry = Physical Chem core
▸Aldehydes + Alcohols + Amines = Organic reaction pattern mastery
▸Coordination Compounds: Maharashtra Board has unique content — don't skip
📐 Mathematics — Blueprint
▸Integration alone = 10–12% · definite integrals asked every year
▸3D Geometry = 11.2% — formulaic, very high ROI for practice
▸Probability + Binomial Distribution = predictable question types
▸Trigonometry (Class XI+XII) = 6% combined — never skip
▸Maths = 100 marks (2 per Q) — dominating Maths = top percentile
📅 Last-Month Revision Roadmap
Week 1
CRITICAL chapters only — Integration, 3D Geometry, Kinetic Theory, Electrostatics, p-Block Elements, Coordination Compounds. Solve 50+ PYQs per chapter from HSC question banks.
Week 2
HIGH-priority chapters — Oscillations & Waves, Rotational Dynamics, Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics, Probability, Differentiation, Vectors. Full HSC textbook re-read for Chemistry.
Week 3
MODERATE + Easy chapters — Semiconductors, Dual Nature, Trigonometry, Mathematical Logic, Alcohols/Aldehydes, AC Circuits, Matrices. Make formula sheets. Take 3 full mocks.
Week 4
Mock tests + Speed drills — Daily full-length mocks (150 Qs in 180 mins). Focus on attempting ALL questions. Analyze errors. Revise formula sheets only. Sleep well. No new topics.
⚡ Quick Score Tips
📐 Maths = 2 marks/Q. Spending extra 30 sec on a Maths question is still worth more than rushing a Physics/Chem question.
⚡ Physics numericals: draw the scenario, write known variables, then apply formula. Never solve in your head.
🧪 Chemistry reactions: memorize reaction products charts for Haloalkanes, Alcohols, Amines. Patterns repeat every year.
🎯 No negative marking = ZERO reason to leave any question blank. Mark your best guess on every question.
📊 Solve Maths paper first — it's worth double marks. If stuck, skip and return; don't waste time on a single question.
📖 Maharashtra State Board textbook Examples + Exercises = 40–50% of CET questions are directly from these.
