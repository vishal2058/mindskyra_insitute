Based on 5-year PYQ analysis, here's an evidence-driven strategy to maximize your JEE Main 2026 score. Focus on high-weightage chapters first, then fill gaps.

⚡ Physics Strategy
▸Master Modern Physics first — highest single-chapter yield (16.7%)
▸Electrostatics + Current Electricity = guaranteed 6–8 marks
▸Optics (Ray + Wave) = ~14% — very reliable scoring zone
▸Never skip Mechanics — Kinematics + Rotational + Laws = ~18%
🧪 Chemistry Strategy
▸NCERT is king — Chemistry is the fastest scoring subject
▸Coordination + d/f-block = highest inorganic yield
▸Physical Chemistry numericals (Electrochemistry + Kinetics + Solutions)
▸Memorize reaction charts for Organic — patterns are predictable
📐 Mathematics Strategy
▸Calculus alone = 27% of Maths — your #1 investment
▸3D Geometry + Vectors = formulaic, very high ROI
▸Matrices & Determinants: easy marks — never leave this unpracticed
▸Maths = 35% of paper — a strong game changes your percentile
📅 Revision Roadmap (Last 4 Weeks)
Week 1
CRITICAL chapters only — Modern Physics, Calculus, Coordination Compounds, Current Electricity, 3D Geometry. Solve 10 PYQs per chapter.
Week 2
HIGH priority chapters — Thermodynamics (both), Electrochemistry, Probability, Differential Equations. Full NCERT revision for Chemistry.
Week 3
MODERATE + formula revision — Matrices, Coordinate Geometry, Magnetic Effects, Wave Optics. Make formula sheets. 2 full mock tests.
Week 4
Mock tests + weak areas — Daily full-length mocks. Analyze mistakes. Revise notes only. Sleep 8 hours. No new topics.
